Who We Are

True Christian Victory Ministry (TCVM) is a Christ-centered church located at Sowutuom VRA, behind the First Block Factory. We are committed to preaching the undiluted Word of God, deliverance, healing, and raising men and women for victorious Christian living.


Our Mandate

To win souls, equip believers, and manifest the victory that Christ has given us through love, faith, and power of the Holy Spirit.
